## The problem
- People in school struggle with managing their time because there is so much to do.
- Impact area: School and learning
- Why it matters (impact): They will have an organized schedule that they can follow.
- Evidence it’s real: The website says "In 2007, parents reported that their high school students spent an average of 7 hours per week on homework. This average does not include the 7 percent of students whose parents reported that they did not do any homework outside school in 2007. About 42 percent of parents reported that their children did homework 5 or more days per week, while 5 percent reported that their children did homework less than once per week. Overall, 65 percent of parents reported that they checked to make sure that their high school students' homework was done." (nces.ed.gov)
- What people told me: Person 1: "This happened to me when I failed to participate properly in class"
"I could make a program to track someone’s homework due tommorow or week"
"I keep track of my homework by using a schedule list and checking homework every time I come home"

## My solution
- My AI’s job: my AI helps by automatically sorting through the tasks and putting them into the best order thats good for your health and other stuff, it also changes when you add something new.
- Tools that already exist, and how mine is different: Mystudylife and Todoist both just let you put your activities somewhere and you also have to pay for the pro versions but my website is completely free and you juse have to enter the things you do and the AI will organize them into the most suitable order for you.
- Safety plan: Physical safety, Wrong advice, Emotional safety, and Bullying or Harmful requests. My website will carefully look through websites and give a warning that ai is NOT ALWAYS ACCURATE, and to fact check it themselves so the user knows its true.

## My MVP features (build these first, in this order)
1. The user can enter tasks and get them sorted into the best order.
2. The user can add a new task and watch the order update.
3. The user can see why the AI put each task in its spot.
4. The AI gives correct, safe answers to real questions.
